# Hero object prompts

## The Velvet Desk — original generated asset

Use case: stylized-concept  
Asset type: transparent hero object for a premium AI consulting and web development agency landing page  
Primary request: Create one extraordinary sculptural object that merges an elegant executive writing desk, a softly draped velvet curtain, and a futuristic portal. The desk should feel physically plausible but surreal: deep oxblood and black-cherry velvet folds cascade over a polished dark smoked-chrome frame, with a thin warm ivory light glowing from inside the portal-like opening beneath the desktop. Subtle glass and brushed metal details suggest advanced technology without screens, circuitry, robots, brains, or generic AI imagery.  
Subject: a single centered velvet desk sculpture, isolated  
Style/medium: ultra-premium editorial product photography, cinematic 3D realism, fashion-campaign art direction, tactile fabric, precise studio rendering  
Composition/framing: wide 16:10 composition, complete object visible, centered slightly right, strong silhouette, plenty of transparent breathing room around all edges, front three-quarter angle at eye level, designed to float over a website background and animate with parallax  
Lighting/mood: dramatic museum spot lighting, warm ivory rim light, soft burgundy bounce, subtle cool graphite shadows, mysterious and sophisticated  
Color palette: oxblood, black cherry, ink black, smoked chrome, warm ivory highlights  
Materials/textures: richly detailed velvet pile and folds, polished smoked metal, restrained glass accents  
Constraints: genuinely transparent alpha background; only one coherent object; no floor, wall, room, text, letters, logo, people, or watermark; clean compositing edges; photorealistic finish  
Avoid: purple neon cyberpunk, blue tech gradients, glowing circuitry, robots, brains, busy particles, stock-photo corporate styling, multiple furniture pieces
