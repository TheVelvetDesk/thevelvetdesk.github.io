### Full event description

# AI for Business

## A Hands-On Guide to Today’s Most Powerful AI Tools

AI is moving fast. This workshop is designed to help you understand what is actually useful, try the tools yourself, and see where they can fit into your work or business. No technical background is required.

We will move beyond ChatGPT and explore the major categories of modern AI through practical demonstrations and guided, hands-on exercises.

### What you will explore

**Text AI and large language models**
Work with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and other leading models. Learn why the same request produces different results and how to give AI the context it needs.

**AI search and research**
Explore Perplexity, NotebookLM, Consensus, and Elicit for research, source-based answers, document analysis, and organizing what you find.

**Image AI**
Generate and compare images across leading models and learn how instructions, references, and style choices affect the result.

**Video AI**
See how today’s frontier video generators compare and where AI video can fit into marketing, training, and creative work.

**Voice, audio, and music**
Explore voice cloning, conversational AI, music generation, and practical audio-editing tools.

**AI coding and app building**
See how a clear idea can become a working website, internal tool, or app—even if you do not write code.

**Automation and AI agents**
Understand the difference between a chatbot, an automation, and an agent that can take action. We will help you begin setting up an agent for day-to-day tasks and show you how to use it responsibly.

**Data and documents**
Use AI to work with PDFs, spreadsheets, reports, and business information.

**Sales, marketing, and customer service**
Explore practical workflows you can bring back to your business for lead research, content, outreach, follow-up, and customer support.

### This workshop is for you if

You are a business owner, professional, entrepreneur, creator, consultant, or simply someone who wants to become comfortable using AI before it becomes an expected part of everyday work.

Complete beginners are welcome. No coding or technical background is required.

### What to bring

- A laptop and charger
- Your curiosity
- One task, process, or idea you would like AI to help you improve

### What you will leave with

- A clearer understanding of the modern AI landscape
- Practical workflows you can use immediately
- First-hand experience with several leading tools
- A better sense of which tools fit your own work
- A take-home AI quick-start guide
